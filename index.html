Research Experience

Soil Moisture Data Fusion and Hybrid Nowcasting

Sponsored research, Aperture Space, Inc.
  • Built a multi-source geospatial soil-moisture dataset: 3.06M station-days of ISMN ground observations (1,599 stations, 56 networks) combined with ERA5-Land/CHIRPS weather, SoilGrids soil properties, SRTM terrain, and ESA WorldCover land cover.
  • Built overpass-aligned panels from 1.44M Sentinel-1 SAR acquisitions, Sentinel-2 optical indices, and MODIS land-surface temperature, using spatial collocation, local-date correction, trailing-only weather windows, quality masks, and acquisition-preserving joins.
  • Designed a hybrid time-series/nowcasting framework that pairs a gradient-boosted current-observation estimate with temporal residual corrections. Implemented a 90-day weather/satellite CNN extension with out-of-fold base predictions, nested station holdouts, and validation-selected fusion weights (multi-seed evaluation in progress).
  • In a PRISM-enhanced LightGBM benchmark, reached 0.0421 m³/m³ median per-station ubRMSE versus 0.0540 for SMAP L4 v007 (22% lower) on 106,584 matched observations from 340 U.S. stations, with station-held-out cross-validation and identical de-biasing.
  • Designed a climatology-relative skill metric, 1 − A/B, comparing model and seasonal-baseline RMSE on identical observations. It uses leave-one-year-out, ±15-day climatology and fixed station-bias correction to measure gains beyond seasonal predictability.

Adaptive Time-Series Tokenization and Forecasting
